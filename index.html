Privacy Policy for Stride

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Stride mobile application (the “App”, package name com.technoorstudio.stride) handles information. Stride is a step and activity tracker developed by TechNoor Studio (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the limited data the App uses.

Stride is designed to work locally on your device. Your step history and personal settings stay on your phone. We do not operate our own servers and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

The following information is created and stored only on your device (in the App’s local database and private app settings). It is never uploaded to us or shared with third parties:

  • Step activity data — your daily step counts, per-day step history, streaks, and active walking time.
  • Body weight (kg) — optional, entered by you and used solely to estimate calories burned.
  • Goals & preferences — your daily step goal, walk-time goal, daily reminder time, chosen accent colour, and onboarding status.

This data is used to display your progress, calculate distance and calories, drive the home-screen widget, and send the reminders and goal alerts you enable. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all of this locally stored data from your device.

2. Device sensors and permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions, each for a specific purpose:

PermissionWhy it is used
Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) To read your device’s built-in step-counter sensor so the App can count your steps. Step readings are processed on-device only.
Foreground service & health service
(FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH)		 To keep counting steps in the background via an ongoing notification, without you needing to keep the App open.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To show the ongoing step-counter notification, optional daily reminders, and the walk-goal alarm.
Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) To resume step tracking automatically after your device restarts.
Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) To deliver your chosen daily reminder at the correct time.
Internet & network state
(INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)		 To load ads and send anonymous analytics and crash reports through the third-party services described below.
Advertising ID (AD_ID) Used by Google AdMob to serve ads. See section 3.

Step-counter and step-detector hardware sensors are optional, so the App remains usable on devices that do not have them.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services provided by Google. These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store your personal health data on any server.

Google AdMob (advertising)

Stride displays ads (app-open, interstitial, and native ads) supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and related information to serve and measure ads. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device’s settings under Google → Ads.

Google Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand, in an aggregated and anonymous way, how the App is used — for example which screens are opened and how often. This helps us improve the App. It does not include your step history, weight, or other personal health data.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic information (such as the type of error and device/OS details) when the App crashes or encounters a handled error. This helps us find and fix stability problems.

For more information about how Google handles data collected through these services, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • How AdMob uses data

4. How your information is used

  • To count your steps and display your daily and 7-day activity, distance, active time, and estimated calories.
  • To provide reminders, goal alerts, and the home-screen widget.
  • To display advertising through Google AdMob.
  • To measure App usage anonymously and to fix crashes, so we can improve the App.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal data. Your on-device step history, weight, and settings are never transmitted to us. The only data leaving your device is the anonymous analytics, crash diagnostics, and advertising data handled by the Google services described in section 3, in accordance with Google’s policies.

6. Data retention

Data stored on your device remains until you delete it within the App (for example by resetting your day) or until you uninstall the App. Data processed by Google’s services is retained according to Google’s own retention policies.

7. Children’s privacy

Stride is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can decline or revoke the Physical activity and Notification permissions in your device settings at any time (some features will stop working).
  • You can reset or limit ad personalisation via your device’s Advertising ID settings.
  • You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

9. Security

Your activity data and settings are stored in the App’s private storage area on your device, which is not accessible to other apps under normal Android security. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
